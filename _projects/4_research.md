Following an Innovation Sprint at Oxford Edge with Babyzone, I am working on developing an app that supports parents to connect with their young children in the in-between moments of daily life, like waiting for a bus.

The app is based in ideas of mindfulness, positive psychology, and parental creativity, using information from the parent and child about where they are and what they notice to generate three connection starters: a story starter, an open-ended question to provoke conversation, and a game idea.

Collaborators on the project include Trisha-mae Capistrano (studying for an MSc in Experimental Psychology) and Dr Jun Zhao (Senior Researcher in Computer Science).
