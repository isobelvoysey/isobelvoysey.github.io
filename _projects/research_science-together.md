From October 2025 to July 2026, I’m working with [Jacari](https://www.jacari.org), a charity who provide English language support to children and young people from refugee, asylum-seeking, and migrant communities in Bristol and Oxford, on a collaborative science project through Science Together.

[Science Together](https://www.mpls.ox.ac.uk/public-engagement/science-together-oxford-researchers-and-communities) is a University of Oxford programme which aims to facilitate mutually beneficial partnerships between researchers at Oxford Brookes and Oxford University and community partners to respond to a local need, challenge or opportunity. 

Jacari’s project aims to learn from the voices of children from refugee, asylum-seeking and migrant communities who receive free English language tutoring, as well as to increase pupils’ and families' feelings of belonging within Jacari and their community. We’re seeking to find the most effective methods and opportunities for engaging the pupils and families using Jacari’s limited capacity and funding.

I’m part of a great team made up of Charlie Payne, a Peer Tutoring Coordinator at Jacari, and our facilitator, Rachel Hindmarsh, a Lecturer and Public Engagement Facilitator at Oxford.
