Developed by a team of researchers from the Oxford Internet Institute, MIT, CMU, and the Oxford Child-Centred AI Lab, Algorithmic Mirror is a tool that reveals the hidden profiles that algorithms create from watch data on platforms like TikTok, Instagram, and Netflix.

In the summer of 2025, I led the user study for this project, conducting interviews with more than 20 teenagers to explore the visualisations created from their platform data and gather their reflections on long-term, cross-platform datafication in their lives.
